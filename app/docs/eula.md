Repository-level copyright and the absence of any public grant are in

app; that file is the notice to everybody who can merely read the source.

## 1. Parties and acceptance

This agreement is between the publisher of Warehouse Advanced ("the Publisher") and the
organisation installing it in its Business Central tenant ("the Customer"). Installing or
using the app constitutes acceptance.

## 2. Licence granted

The Publisher grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence to
install and use Warehouse Advanced in the Business Central tenants covered by the Customer's
agreement with the Publisher, for the Customer's own internal business operations.

The app is supplied as a **per-tenant extension**. It is not licensed for redistribution and
is not offered through Microsoft AppSource.

## 3. Restrictions

The Customer may not:

- distribute, sublicense, sell, rent or lend the app or any part of it to a third party;
- use it to provide a service to a third party outside the Customer's own operations,
  unless separately agreed in writing;
- remove or alter copyright notices, the publisher name, or the object affix;
- reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the compiled app, except to the extent that
  applicable law expressly permits despite this restriction.

**Reading the source is not a restricted act.** The source is published, and the app permits
debugging and source download in the Customer's own tenant, precisely so the Customer can
audit and support what runs in their environment. What is restricted is redistribution and
reuse, not inspection.

## 4. Ownership

The Publisher retains all right, title and interest in the app, including all intellectual
property in it. The Customer owns its own data. Nothing in this agreement transfers ownership
of either.

## 5. Customer data

The app stores warehouse operational data inside the Customer's own Business Central tenant
and transmits nothing to the Publisher or to any third party. See the
[privacy statement](privacy-statement.md).

Where the Customer configures the app's integration surface to exchange messages with another
system, responsibility for what is sent, and to whom, rests with the Customer.

## 9. Term and termination

This licence runs for as long as the Customer's agreement with the Publisher is in force. It
terminates if the Customer materially breaches it and does not remedy the breach within a
reasonable period after being notified.

On termination the Customer must uninstall the app. The Customer's own data stays in the
Customer's tenant; the app does not take it away, and uninstalling does not require the
Customer to surrender anything.
